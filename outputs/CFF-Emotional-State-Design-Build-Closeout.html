Twelve design/build specifications

The values below are the coordinated brief for architecture, landscape, interiors, MEP, digital systems and operations.

ES-001

Safety

FIXED + SITE VERIFY

Design intent

Threat detection can quiet before any higher state is attempted.

Spatial contract

Protected back or flank at every pause; next safe destination visible before refuge ends; zero cabin-to-cabin sightlines; guest and operational paths physically separated.

Design/build values

  • Zero unplanned guest/operative crossings
  • No concealed door opening into guest path
  • Night luminance transition ≤ 1:10
  • Emergency information explicit

Zone

Z0–Z4 / all occupied territory

IoT authority

Access state, route-clear state and operational-route lock. No cameras, microphones or emotion inference.

Evidence

Day/night/monsoon first-time route test; standing/seated/reclined privacy matrix; 30-day crossing soak.

Failure and recovery

Expose human assistance immediately when safety is uncertain; safety overrides invisible-service choreography.

ES-002

Relief

FIXED / TARGET

Design intent

Obligations and arrival logistics become demonstrably unreachable.

Spatial contract

One-way sensory descent; protected compression followed by release; rest and thermal affordance before explanation or choice.

Design/build values

  • Road-to-sanctuary sound: ~50 → 35 → <30 dBA
  • Night-one light: 2200 K / 50 lux
  • Arrival active decisions: 0
  • Design target: first long exhale ≤12 min*

Zone

Z0 Severance → HAM-01 court

IoT authority

Portal readiness, arrival scene and pace-timed tub heat; all actions reversible and pre-verified.

Evidence

Four-condition arrival rehearsal; gradient readings; readiness trace; zero-task audit. *Behavioural target, not guarantee.

Failure and recovery

Remain in safety-support mode, remove demand and suppress progression; never add performance or ritual.

ES-003

Orientation

FIXED + P-R0

Design intent

The place is legible without an instructional burden.

Spatial contract

One dominant route; cue visible one walking beat before junction; material, ground, sound and warm-light grammar consistent; accessible route preserves the same sequence.

Design/build values

  • No instructional signage in Z1/Z3/Z4
  • Maximum 2 plausible choices at unavoidable junction
  • Provisional hesitation defect: >3 s or route reversal
  • 100% emergency egress clarity

Zone

Z1 Threshold / Z3 movement network

IoT authority

Restrained path-light availability and accessible fallback; digital navigation is not the primary mechanism.

Evidence

Naive-user trials in daylight, darkness, rain and fog; wrong-turn and hesitation log; accessibility verification.

Failure and recovery

Provide dignified assistance and record a spatial defect; do not repair ambiguous geometry with brand signage.

ES-004

Stillness

FIXED + P-R0

Design intent

Motor and cognitive demand can cease without removing agency.

Spatial contract

Rest place outside circulation; no display or merchandise in primary field; excellent defaults; long-view event density deliberately low.

Design/build values

  • Zero active decisions 21:00–09:00
  • Sleeping NC ≤25
  • Blackout ≤1 lux
  • Provisional uninterrupted dwell field ≥30 min

Zone

HAM-02/HAM-03 sanctuary and protected night

IoT authority

Quiet mode, non-critical alert suppression and stable plant control. Manual exit and override always available.

Evidence

Decision-load audit; occupied dwell rehearsal; scene-cycle and plant-noise tests; service-incursion simulation.

Failure and recovery

Suppress prompts and inspect unstable environment; do not instruct the occupant to relax.

ES-005

Solitude

FIXED + SITE VERIFY

Design intent

Aloneness remains chosen because connection is available and optional.

Spatial contract

No intervisibility at standing, seated, bathing or sleeping datums; operational paths outside acoustic territory; commons reachable by a short legible route.

Design/build values

  • Zero sanctuary-to-sanctuary sightlines
  • Speech unintelligible across private territories
  • Privacy valid leaf-on, leaf-off and night
  • No blind required for baseline privacy

Zone

Private deck / court / sleeping territory

IoT authority

Protect route and delivery boundaries using asset and workflow state—not behavioural surveillance.

Evidence

360° privacy model; night silhouette test; speech privacy test; seasonal planting and service-route audit.

Failure and recovery

Protect privacy immediately; suspend conflicting route or inventory; correct landscape/geometry before release.

ES-006

Sensory acuity

FIXED

Design intent

Small authentic signals become perceptible as artificial load falls.

Spatial contract

Low mechanical and visual noise; three primary materials per space; one dominant light event; real water, rain, coffee, soil and timber at source.

Design/build values

  • Outdoor sanctuary <30 dBA
  • Indoor masking systems prohibited
  • ≤3 primary materials per space
  • Rill switchable where it becomes demand

Zone

HAM-02/HAM-03 / landscape movement

IoT authority

Monitor plant/environmental noise and operate water only within approved acoustic envelope.

Evidence

Octave and intermittency survey; visual-noise audit; scent/source provenance; structured non-leading observation.

Failure and recovery

Remove artificial stimulus; never add soundscape or fragrance to imitate absent place.

ES-007

Awe

SITE VERIFY

Design intent

A single safe encounter with scale follows prolonged visual withholding.

Spatial contract

Dominant horizon concealed on earlier routes; final turn reveals one frame; standing datum aligns with horizon; no brand, caption, bench or narration.

Design/build values

  • Earlier guest sightline ≤10 m
  • One primary far-horizon reveal
  • Weather and safety veto always active
  • No commercial view-category premium

Zone

HAM-05 Harvest Ridge aperture

IoT authority

Safe-route readiness and minimum necessary wayfinding light only; no “awe detection.”

Evidence

Seasonal view cone; full-scale aperture mock-up; approach film; fog/rain/wind safety test; leak audit.

Failure and recovery

Withhold the reveal when unsafe or optically compromised; never fabricate a spectacle.

ES-008

Intimacy

PVE-02 APPROVAL

Design intent

Attention can return to the person present without assuming relationship or consent.

Spatial contract

Adjacent seating with shared outward focus; conversation outside circulation; third focus such as fire, pour or horizon; equal-quality solo use.

Design/build values

  • Low-voice speech private at adjacent routes
  • No screen in primary shared field
  • Ritual always optional
  • Controls independently accessible

Zone

Shared deck / fire / pour / bath settings

IoT authority

Offer shared environmental presets without classifying relationships or conversational behaviour.

Evidence

Speech privacy; interruption audit; body-dimension mock-up; solo and varied-pair usability tests.

Failure and recovery

Remove imposed romantic cues or unequal control; preserve personal privacy and refusal.

ES-009

Agency

FIXED

Design intent

Automation removes logistics while leaving authorship, refusal and exit intact.

Spatial contract

Immediate manual override for light, access, privacy and safe thermal control; declining a ritual never reduces base quality.

Design/build values

  • ≤3 active decisions/day, all delegable
  • 0 decisions 21:00–09:00
  • Manual override response ≤1 s local
  • Basic use never requires an app

Zone

All controlled environments

IoT authority

Only reversible actions inside an approved envelope; least privilege; local fallback; explicit stop conditions.

Evidence

End-to-end decision inventory; refusal, override, offline and accessibility tests; quarterly commercial audit.

Failure and recovery

Return control locally, stop automation and create a human recovery task when command state is ambiguous.

ES-010

Restoration

FIXED

Design intent

The physical conditions for sleep and physiological recovery are continuously available.

Spatial contract

Acoustic mass, blackout, stable humidity and air, circadian light, thermal ritual and sufficient duration work as one system.

Design/build values

  • Sleeping NC ≤25
  • Field ASTC ≥65
  • RH 50–55% / CO₂ <800 ppm
  • Pillow air ≤0.1 m/s
  • 4000 K dawn → 2200 K dusk
  • Evening 50–80 lux / flicker <5%

Zone

Sleeping, bathing and thermal-ritual envelope

IoT authority

Edge control of RH, air, plant, light and water; automatic correction remains inside commissioned ranges.

Evidence

G1/G2/G3/G4/G8/G9 per hamlet; 24 h monsoon soak; no averaging and no partial release.

Failure and recovery

Treat threshold breach as a technical incident; protect sleep, notify discreetly and never infer a health outcome.

ES-011

Gratitude

FIXED

Design intent

Value may be felt without a final transactional demand.

Spatial contract

Continuous exit without desk, queue, retail or payment surface; luggage moves ahead; last threshold closes quietly.

Design/build values

  • Departure active decisions: 0
  • Visible price/review/tip prompts: 0
  • Unplanned waiting: 0
  • Thanks never scored

Zone

Final morning / aperture → gate

IoT authority

Silent settlement readiness, luggage workflow and departure event; legal exception routes remain available.

Evidence

End-to-end departure rehearsal; zero-asks log; failed-settlement recovery test; accessibility review.

Failure and recovery

Resolve practical/legal needs openly and respectfully; never protect theatre at the expense of the person.

ES-012

Anticipation

PVE-02 APPROVAL

Design intent

A real, unconsumed season or possibility leaves return imaginable without pursuit.

Spatial contract

Do not expose every hamlet, ritual, clearing or season; retrieval object comes from the experienced material loop.

Design/build values

  • Home ritual ≤2 min
  • Push reminders/streaks/badges: 0
  • One-tap return optional
  • Seasonal claim requires operating evidence

Zone

Departure / carryover / later season

IoT authority

Consented optional continuity; minimal retention; no behavioural pressure or automated emotional targeting.

Evidence

Object provenance; content and privacy audit; seasonal operations verification; retention and deletion test.

Failure and recovery

Stop messaging and delete unauthorised continuity; never turn carryover into merchandise or engagement.
