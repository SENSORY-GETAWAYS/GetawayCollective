PVE-02 · Property-native state machine · R1

Between Two Waters

Not twelve universal emotions wearing coastal finishes. A state machine that could only have been produced by this neck, this salt, this monsoon and this confluence.

The SG twelve are retained only as a governance coverage vocabulary. They no longer dictate SSC’s visible sequence, names, count or form.
01 · Corrected design philosophy

The property writes the states

A real property is not required to visit twelve emotional nouns in a canonical order. SSC has nine native conditions, one weather-dependent branch and a three-night temporal threshold. Some conditions may support several human effects; some effects may never occur. The design obligation is to create truthful conditions, not to force an emotional checklist.

01

Phenomena before labels

Neck, pebble, darkness, salt, horizons, monsoon and tide are causal design material.

02

Branches, not conveyor belts

A dry sunset and a monsoon cell produce different valid sequences. Refusal is another valid branch.

03

Duration has structure

Tide Anchor is expected only after withdrawal and dilation. Arrival calm is not the final product.

04

No emotional automation

Systems maintain conditions and survive faults. They do not detect, score or manufacture inner life.

SSC DESIGN LAWIf a state could be moved unchanged to a forest, coffee estate or desert, it is not an SSC state. It is merely a generic outcome label.
02 · State machine

Road-time becomes tide-time

The main path is spatial, the deep transition is temporal, and weather may author a branch. The machine is deliberately uneven.

Dry sunset

Double Horizon → Salt Dilation → Estuary Hush

Sea light falls; amber takes over; tributary becomes the private clock.

Monsoon cell

Double Horizon → Monsoon Absorption → Estuary Hush

The roof, drip line and hearth replace the sunset sequence.

Clear dawn

Estuary Hush → optional tributary contact → Tide Anchor

Edge access remains conditional on CRZ and water-safety release.

Infrastructure fault

Current state → Quiet Hold → human recovery

Local comfort persists; no reboot, notification or visible technical drama.

Explicit refusal

Any optional offer → current state continues

Refusal completes the interaction; no second offer or substitute programme.

03 · Native SSC states

Nine conditions, not twelve boxes

Each state is named from a property phenomenon and includes the transition it causes, evidence that can be built and the cheap imitation that must be prohibited.

SSC-00

ENTRY CONDITION

Roadmind

Before the bronze gate
Beach Road / external world
The arriving body is fast, scanning and cognitively occupied. SSC does not pretend calm already exists.

Property instruments

The gate withholds the estate. No preview image, illuminated sign or visible lobby competes with the road. The narrow neck is accepted as the first design fact.

Transition

The gate opens only into a surface that makes road-speed physically impossible.

Proof

Arrival baseline records gait, vehicle speed, questions and visible scanning as design research—not as individual diagnosis.

Cheap imitation prohibited

Do not greet with a brand speech, welcome screen, fragrance, music or a menu of activities.

SSC-01

KINETIC STATE

Pebble Surrender

Minutes 0–7
Z0 · 58 m access neck
Control is relinquished first through the tyres and feet, not through instruction.

Property instruments

River pebble 40–60 mm, 100 mm deep; one-car width; forward view broken twice; bronze gate behind; no sign telling anyone to slow down.

Transition

Asphalt → loose pebble. Road rhythm becomes crawl rhythm before any human contact.

Proof

Ten-car uninstructed trial ≤10 km/h; walking cadence visibly reduces; fire access and accessible transfer remain independently resolved.

Cheap imitation prohibited

Do not substitute a speed sign, scripted welcome, valet performance or decorative gravel strip.

SSC-02

SEVERANCE STATE

Dark Interval

Minutes 7–12
Arrival court → Z1 airlock
The outside world is not softened; it is interrupted. The interval has no view, transaction or social obligation.

Property instruments

Windowless 2.0 × 3.5 m chamber; ≥10:1 luminance collapse within ≤4 m; ≥15 dB(A) acoustic shear; −2.0 K ±0.5; 1.2 kg bronze pull; luggage disappears.

Transition

The body crosses a measurable absence. Authentication, forms and settlement have happened elsewhere.

Proof

Uninstructed route contains no stop, question, signature or screen. Light, sound and temperature deltas are commissioned together.

Cheap imitation prohibited

Do not turn the darkness into theatre with projection, voiceover, scent, artwork or a host waiting at the end.

SSC-03

RELEASE STATE

Double Horizon

First emergence
Z2 · coastal plaza
The property is understood as a narrow country between two waters—not as a building with a sea view.

Property instruments

Release volume ≥3:1; seated sea aperture ≥120°; sunset held on the Arabian Sea side; tributary withheld for the private field; pale-sage water reflects rather than advertises leisure.

Transition

Compression gives scale back. North becomes social and solar; south remains private and tidal.

Proof

Seated-view survey protects both readings; first-time movement is legible without commercial signage; glare and horizon blockage are tested after furniture placement.

Cheap imitation prohibited

Do not expose both waters at once, make the pool turquoise, add a photo marker or convert the plaza into a reception lounge.

SSC-04

ATTENTIONAL STATE

Salt Dilation

First afternoon → amber shift
Plaza / garden gap / west veranda
Attention widens because artificial information falls below the coast’s real signals.

Property instruments

Kavi underfoot; matte concrete and bronze; salt air; casuarina movement; no visible light source; after 17:30 only amber grazing; one bronze dial carries the room’s explicit choices.

Transition

The occupant stops searching for programme and begins receiving low-intensity coastal information.

Proof

≤10 GU floor, ≤15 GU millwork, 1800 K exterior base light, glare review and no default screen. Unprompted noticing may be recorded qualitatively.

Cheap imitation prohibited

No playlist, scent delivery, digital itinerary, decorative “coastal” objects, circadian claim or attention score.

SSC-05

WEATHER BRANCH

Monsoon Absorption

When the cell arrives
Deep roof / drip line / monsoon veranda
Rain is not bad weather SSC tolerates. It is the estate’s largest spatial event and it temporarily rewrites the sequence.

Property instruments

Four thousand millimetres designed for; warm drop before the cell; hearth behind; 2° plate makes one rain sheet; roof sound occupies the masking band; scuppers carry 100% downpipe blockage.

Transition

Dry-day movement branches into protected exposure: weather becomes screen, soundtrack and clock.

Proof

1:1 mock-up in real driven rain; safe refuge, drainage, lightning and egress test; no mangrove spill; roof overflow witnessed under blockage.

Cheap imitation prohibited

No umbrella performance, storm soundtrack, projection, weather narration or forced attendance. On a dry day this state does not occur.

SSC-06

PRIVATE RELEASE

Estuary Hush

First night / each dawn
Z4 · tributary-facing studio
The tributary is not the second view. It is the quieter clock to which the private world is tuned.

Property instruments

Bed mass hides the entry; centre held empty; twin wet/dress tracks compress once more; 0.0 mm threshold releases to a private south balcony; 32° fins remove adjacent eyes; mangrove line receives zero light.

Transition

Social sea scale folds into tidal privacy. Company remains optional and reachable, never ambient.

Proof

Zero inter-key sightlines by night and day; STC ≥60 intent; NC-25 pillow; 0.0 lux capability; no-knock hatch and 72-hour zero-intersection audit.

Cheap imitation prohibited

No shared corridor encounter, staff silhouette, voice assistant, camera, microphone capsule or illuminated mangrove edge.

SSC-07

TEMPORAL STATE

Tide Anchor

Night 2 → night 3
Sanctuary / soak / balcony
SSC’s decisive state is not relaxation on arrival. It is the point when the building stops feeling new and tide replaces schedule.

Property instruments

21 °C sleep basis; silent air; 37 °C private water; NC-25; 0.0 lux; secondary circuits die at 21:00; no clock; silent deliveries; architecture repeats dependable conditions while the coast changes.

Transition

Night 1 withdrawal → night 2 dilation → night 3 anchor. The temporal arc cannot be compressed into a one-night product.

Proof

Per-key overnight environmental trace; hour-36 heavy-bathing scenario; local controls survive WAN/core loss; unhurried dwell may be observed without biometric proof.

Cheap imitation prohibited

No sleep score, wellness promise, HRV requirement, countdown, morning push notification or staff interruption disguised as care.

SSC-08

EXIT CONDITION

Unfinished Coast

Final morning / beyond the gate
Tributary dawn → Z0 departure
Departure must not close the world into a completed consumption. One real season, tide or weather condition remains unavailable and therefore imaginable.

Property instruments

Last view is place, not brand; luggage and settlement are resolved outside the private field; no review surface; seasonal absence is truthful; any memory object is local, useful and unbranded.

Transition

The estate recedes without a sales climax. Return may become imaginable because the coast continues without the occupant.

Proof

Zero-solicitation departure audit; no unresolved logistics; future-season language appears only when freely initiated; separate consent for any continuity.

Cheap imitation prohibited

No review request, scarcity message, loyalty mechanic, branded souvenir, sentiment detection or behavioural retargeting from property telemetry.

04 · Governance translation

The SG twelve become coverage

The enterprise vocabulary remains useful for checking whether an important human dimension has been ignored. It must not prescribe the property’s state names, sequence, quantity or architectural expression.

Coverage is not choreography.

A single native state may support several SG dimensions. One SG dimension may appear in several native states. None is guaranteed to occur internally.
