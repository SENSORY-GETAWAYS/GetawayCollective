This is a source-grounded PVE-02 design map. It creates spatial conditions for emotional states; it does not guarantee, diagnose or score them.
01 · Design philosophy

The river teaches slowness

Creek is movement and seclusion. The stream is not scenery: it masks, cools, times, orients and sequences the estate. Architecture stays subordinate to water, canopy and relationship.

01

Recovery first

The estate subtracts. Nothing is introduced that requires processing.

02

Silence moves

Silence is not absence here; it is current, rain, rill and low natural information.

03

Attention is side-by-side

Shared views and angled bodies rebuild attention before eye-to-eye performance.

04

Wet is the true state

Materials and elevations are designed and approved soaked, weathered and alive.

05

Technology withdraws

Presence, light and temperature may be sensed locally. Identity, speech and emotion may not.

One-sentence positionSlowSpace Creek is the river-forest estate where two people disappear for three nights and come back to each other.
02 · Four-movement map

From rapids to return

The twelve SG states are composed into four Creek movements. The emotional sequence follows water’s behaviour rather than a hotel programme.

I · Z0–Z1

SEVERANCE

Safety → Relief

Rapids — turbulence, then the drop

Arrival descent; external identity set down; −5 to −12 BPM target over the 12-minute arc.

II · Z2–Z3

CONFLUENCE

Orientation → Intimacy → Agency

Two currents joining

Fire, heat and water let two people move from coordination back to attention.

III · Z4

STILLWATER

Solitude → Stillness → Acuity → Restoration

The deep dark pool

Sovereign isolation, sleep repair and unhurried attention with nothing scheduled.

IV · Z6 + departure

RETURN

Awe → Gratitude → Anticipation

The river continuing without you

Crossing, whole sky, living artifact and closure without ceremony.

Spatial reading

Long axis

The route itself is the instrument: distance lowers speed before programme begins.

Dispersed keys

Studios sit as independent stillwater territories rather than room corridors.

Creek threshold

The watercourse is a real ecological and emotional boundary; flood verification governs.

Confluence precinct

Commons, heat and water provide optional togetherness after sovereignty is established.

Return landscape

Working yard, whole sky and planting turn departure into useful closure.

03 · Property emotional state map

Twelve Creek states

Each state is grounded in a Creek territory, a physical instrument, an IoT boundary and observable evidence that avoids psychological diagnosis.

ES-001

Safety

Severance

Spatial territory

Z0–Z1 arrival and Ranger handoff

Design philosophy

Closed canopy, protected arrival wall, one human handoff and no vehicle beyond the gate.

Physical instruments

Laterite, river-cast concrete, warm low light.

IoT boundary

No unplanned encounter; route and water-edge readiness.

Evidence without diagnosis

Threat scanning subsides; no wayfinding request.

ES-002

Relief

Severance

Spatial territory

Arrival descent → compression corridor

Design philosophy

External identity is set down through a 12-minute descending arc; sound, light, decisions and speed reduce together.

Physical instruments

Rapids heard first, 2200 K knee-height light, cobble-to-trowelled threshold.

IoT boundary

Arrival scene, route-clear state and room-readiness only.

Evidence without diagnosis

First long exhale within 12 minutes as a design target, not a guarantee.

ES-003

Orientation

Confluence

Spatial territory

Z2 commons / Z3 water instruments

Design philosophy

The rill, one brick-dust warm line, level change and material threshold replace directional signage.

Physical instruments

Water sound, brick dust, river basalt and the Ranger handoff.

IoT boundary

Path light and safe fallback; no behavioural tracking.

Evidence without diagnosis

First-time movement without hesitation; statutory information remains explicit.

ES-008

Intimacy

Confluence

Spatial territory

Hearth, brew bar, sauna deck, coracle

Design philosophy

Two nervous systems synchronise side-by-side before face-to-face attention is expected.

Physical instruments

Paired chairs converge 30° toward water or fire; shared view and low-voice privacy.

IoT boundary

Offer HEARTH/CURRENT conditions without relationship inference.

Evidence without diagnosis

Conversation and shared ritual can occur without operational interruption.

ES-009

Agency

Confluence

Spatial territory

All ritual choice points

Design philosophy

Rituals are offered once and quietly; refusal completes the interaction; solo participation is equal.

Physical instruments

One state dial, one hush control, no screen or indicator.

IoT boundary

Reversible explicit choices; local manual override; offline-safe.

Evidence without diagnosis

Plans may change without permission or penalty.

ES-005

Solitude

Stillwater

Spatial territory

Z4 studio, deck, mirror pool

Design philosophy

Sovereign isolation remains chosen because commons and help are safely reachable.

Physical instruments

Short canopy horizon, private water edge and ghost-side service.

IoT boundary

Presence supports room readiness only; never identity or surveillance.

Evidence without diagnosis

Private territory remains visually and acoustically independent.

ES-004

Stillness

Stillwater

Spatial territory

Sleep core / stream deck / Rain Sit

Design philosophy

The estate withdraws; no schedule, prompt, display or unnecessary service remains.

Physical instruments

NC-25 sleep core, ≤0.5 lux pillow darkness, rain under 900 mm eave.

IoT boundary

STILLWATER mode; extended blackout; quiet plant; single-party windows.

Evidence without diagnosis

Unhurried dwell without reaching for a task.

ES-006

Sensory acuity

Stillwater

Spatial territory

Gallery, deck and rain interfaces

Design philosophy

Reduced artificial load makes current, rain, moss, woodsmoke and wet material perceptible.

Physical instruments

Riverbed palette; matte wet-state surfaces; stream as real acoustic instrument.

IoT boundary

Lux, temperature and environmental conditions only; no synthetic soundscape.

Evidence without diagnosis

Small authentic signals are noticed without prompting.

ES-010

Restoration

Stillwater

Spatial territory

Sleep core / sauna / plunge / onsen

Design philosophy

Sleep repair and thermal regulation happen within a stable, silent envelope.

Physical instruments

NC-25; STC-60 design basis; 18.5–21 °C sleep band; sauna 80 °C; water sequence controlled.

IoT boundary

Local environmental control with safety limits and manual exit.

Evidence without diagnosis

Commissioned physical thresholds pass per key; no health claim inferred.

ES-007

Awe

Return

Spatial territory

Z6 crossing / Star Court / wildlife corridor

Design philosophy

Scale returns through moving water, whole sky, monsoon and the passage of non-human life—not a staged viewpoint.

Physical instruments

One bridge, dark-sky court, rain curtain, canopy and river movement.

IoT boundary

Safe access, water/weather state and wildlife protocol; no spectacle automation.

Evidence without diagnosis

Silence or stillness may emerge; safety and ecological distance govern.

ES-011

Gratitude

Return

Spatial territory

Final morning / Sapling / departure

Design philosophy

Closure is useful rather than ceremonial: a living contribution replaces a branded souvenir.

Physical instruments

Two hands set a root ball; luggage and settlement remain outside the field.

IoT boundary

Readiness and provenance only; gratitude is never detected or scored.

Evidence without diagnosis

Departure contains no solicitation, tip prompt or review request.

ES-012

Anticipation

Return

Spatial territory

Post-threshold / future season

Design philosophy

The river continues without the occupant; an anniversary, monsoon or growing tree leaves return imaginable.

Physical instruments

GPS-tagged sapling, changing water, seasonal ritual and honest date ranges.

IoT boundary

Consented optional continuity; no streaks, urgency or behavioural retargeting.

Evidence without diagnosis

Future season or return may be named without prompting.
