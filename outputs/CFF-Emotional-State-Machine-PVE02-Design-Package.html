Artifact 01B · Spatial performance specification

Twelve states, twelve spatial contracts

Each contract defines the conditions architecture must make available. A technical pass proves the instrument is present; behavioural observation tests whether it is working; neither authorises a claim that every person will feel the same thing.

Causality rule
Specify inputs precisely. Observe responses ethically. Never treat posture, physiology or behaviour as consent, diagnosis or proof of an inner emotional state.
ES-001

Safety

Threat detection can quiet

Spatial organisation

  • Arrival route has one legible direction and no blind decision junction.
  • Every pause position provides back or flank protection with an outward view.
  • Guest and operational routes are physically separated before the first threshold.

Geometry & sightlines

  • Approach preview: next safe destination visible before current refuge ends.
  • No concealed door opens into the guest movement envelope.
  • Zero cabin-to-cabin sightline at standing, seated and sleeping eye datums.

Sensory hardpoints

  • No sudden luminance jump greater than 1:10 on the arrival path.
  • No intermittent plant noise, alarms or unexplained mechanical actuation in normal mode.
  • Hand-contact materials are stable, dry and thermally non-startling.

CFF application

Board-marked severance wall, Robusta blinders, portal opening before arrival, no gate conversation, one warm route, luggage removed from the visible sequence, steam visible before any human encounter.

TRACE: Z0/Z1 · HAM-01 · M-01/M-03/M-07 · ES-001 precedes every other state

Validation

Day/night first-time-user walk; leaf-off and monsoon sightline audit; operational crossing simulation; structured observation of hesitation and scanning. Record technical defects, not psychological labels.

Failure / HOLD

Unexpected staff appearance, ambiguous path, unshielded service door, unseen vehicle conflict, cabin visibility or an unexplained sound source. Spatial release stops until eliminated.

ES-002

Relief

The held breath may release

Sequence

  • Create an unmistakable boundary between road condition and estate condition.
  • Reduce acoustic, visual, tactile and decision intensity monotonically.
  • Deliver a restorative affordance before explanation or orientation demand.

Section & pace

  • Compression at threshold, followed by protected release—not an exposed grand reveal.
  • Arrival walking duration is calibrated to depletion and accessibility.
  • Rest opportunity appears before any programme choice.

Performance

  • Road-to-forest sound decline: approximately 50 → 35 → <30 dBA.
  • Night-one scene: 2200 K, approximately 50 lux, floor pooled.
  • Water/thermal ritual ready on arrival without a control task.

CFF application

Gravel Reach takes the first night. The portal removes negotiation; the shortest hamlet approach limits effort; densest planting terminates external visual demand; the pace-timed tub makes steam—not staff—the welcome.

TARGET: first long exhale usually within 12 minutes; never advertised as guaranteed

Validation

Time-stamped arrival simulation across dry, monsoon, night and mobility scenarios; measure gradient limbs and task count; compare design variants without displaying scores to the occupant.

Failure / recovery

Check-in, waiting, luggage handling, control explanation, choice prompt or thermal ritual not ready. Recover by removing demand; do not compensate with performative hospitality.

ES-003

Orientation

Legibility without instruction

Wayfinding grammar

  • Use ground texture, enclosure, sound and warm-light hierarchy as a consistent language.
  • One dominant route at each decision point; secondary routes read as unavailable or private.
  • Landmarks repeat by function, not decoration.

Decision geometry

  • Destination cue appears at least one walking beat before the junction.
  • No more than two plausible choices at an unavoidable junction.
  • Accessible path preserves the same emotional sequence and dignity.

Information limits

  • No instructional signage in Z1, Z3 or Z4.
  • Emergency and statutory information remains explicit and never subordinated to brand minimalism.
  • Digital navigation is fallback, not primary design.

CFF application

Laterite becomes gravel, gravel becomes granite setts, setts become earth and boardwalk. The rill and pooled light run with the guest axis. Hamlet entries use distinct microclimate and planting cues while keeping the same route grammar.

PASS: first-time movement without hesitation or a wayfinding question

Validation

Naive-user trials by daylight, blackout, rain and fog; record wrong turns and hesitation locations; test accessibility and emergency egress separately.

Failure / HOLD

A sign is proposed to repair ambiguous geometry; two warm routes compete; operations require verbal directions; accessible circulation becomes a visibly secondary journey.

ES-005

Solitude

Alone, with connection available

Privacy envelope

  • No intervisibility between sanctuary interiors, tubs or primary private decks.
  • Approach routes do not pass through another sanctuary's acoustic or visual territory.
  • Ghost service does not create silhouettes, shadows or latch noise.

Connection condition

  • The commons remains reachable by a short, legible walk.
  • Distant signs of collective life may be perceived without speech becoming intelligible.
  • Private withdrawal never resembles confinement; exit remains obvious.

Landscape mechanism

  • Botanical screening uses layered height, seasonal density and oblique geometry.
  • Fences and opaque defensive devices do not substitute for landscape composition.
  • Privacy proof covers leaf-off, maintenance gaps and night lighting.

CFF application

Robusta and pepper-vine walls, staggered studio placement, ten-metre sightline control and private decks oriented toward living landscape—not another room. Civic Heart remains optional and discoverable.

STATE DISTINCTION: solitude requires a visible choice; isolation removes it

Validation

360-degree standing/seated/reclined view matrix; night silhouette test; speech privacy test; route-crossing model; seasonal planting audit.

Failure / HOLD

Another occupant, operative, phone light or service reflection becomes visible; private deck faces an unavoidable social route; guest must close a blind to obtain baseline privacy.

ES-004

Stillness

Motor demand can cease

Demand subtraction

  • No display, menu, itinerary or merchandise occupies the primary rest field.
  • Controls default correctly; override is available but not visually solicitous.
  • Seating supports multiple resting postures without prescribed use.

Spatial duration

  • At least one place supports a 60-minute dwell without circulation conflict.
  • Views have slow natural variation rather than high visual event density.
  • No staff task requires entry into the stillness envelope.

Night constitution

  • Zero active decisions and guest-facing contact 21:00–09:00.
  • Sleeping NC-25 with all normal plant operating.
  • Blackout ≤1 lux with safe, non-intrusive egress available.

CFF application

Mist Hollow lengthens fades by 20%; Stream Terrace uses the slowest rill; Night Pour is permitted but not programmed. The room contains no screen and no object asking to be used.

AUDIT: every prompt, display, notification and service cue is eliminated, defaulted or delegated

Validation

Decision-load audit; 60-minute occupied dwell test; after-dark scene cycle; plant-noise test; housekeeping/service incursion simulation.

Failure / recovery

Any prompt framed as helpful; unstable HVAC; notification light; forced ritual; seating on a movement path; service timing the occupant must manage.

ES-006

Sensory acuity

Small signals become available

Noise floor

  • Remove broadband mechanical masking indoors; preserve low-level natural signals.
  • Water is tuned as an outdoor instrument and switchable where it becomes demand.
  • Intermittent events are controlled more strictly than average level alone.

Visual field

  • Limit materials to three primary surfaces per space.
  • Keep luminance composition legible: one dominant light event, no competing accents.
  • Frame near nature before far scenery.

Olfactory / tactile

  • Use real coffee, rain, timber, earth and fire at their actual source.
  • No ambient fragrance used to repair absent place.
  • Ground and hand-contact textures change slowly enough to be noticed.

CFF application

Fog Walk makes rain chains audible before architecture is visible; Stream Terrace lowers the acoustic floor; blossom and roast appear seasonally and truthfully; board-marked concrete, stoneware and coffee work return attention to touch.

PRINCIPLE: the environment does not add stimulation; it lowers the threshold of perception

Validation

Octave-band and intermittency survey; visual-noise audit; source verification for scent; structured report of noticed signals without leading questions.

Failure / HOLD

Soundscape playback replaces real acoustic design; scent diffuser substitutes for ecology; decorative lighting competes with daylight; operational noise erases small signals.

ES-009

Agency

Authorship without logistics

Choice architecture

  • Choices are reversible, delegable and meaningful—not configuration labour.
  • Defaults are safe and excellent; declining participation never degrades the base condition.
  • Controls communicate effect through placement and response, not instruction density.

Physical control

  • Immediate manual override exists for light, privacy, access and thermal safety.
  • No autonomy layer locks the occupant into a scene or ritual.
  • Accessible controls are reachable, operable and perceivable without assistance.

Decision budget

  • Maximum three active decisions per day, all delegable.
  • No choice presented during the protected night window.
  • New commercial or operational touchpoints must replace—not add to—the budget.

CFF application

No room selection, check-in, housekeeping scheduling, checkout or temperature management. The meaningful surviving choices are whether to eat, whether to take a ritual window and whether to claim a folly.

CONTROL LAW: automation removes logistics but never removes exit, refusal or override

Validation

End-to-end decision inventory; refusal-path testing; manual-override and fail-safe test; accessibility review; quarterly audit against commercial additions.

Failure / HOLD

Choice disguised as consent; app required for basic use; preset cannot be exited; automation anticipates consequential preferences; refusal produces lesser spatial quality.

ES-008

Intimacy

Attention can return to the person present

Dyadic geometry

  • Provide adjacent seating with shared outward focus, not forced face-to-face performance.
  • Conversation zones sit outside circulation and service sightlines.
  • Acoustic absorption and distance protect low-voice speech.

Shared ritual

  • Objects support two-person participation without scripted roles.
  • Fire, pour, bath or horizon provides a third focus that reduces social pressure.
  • Ritual can be declined without explanation.

Competing channels

  • No screen in the primary shared field.
  • Personal device charging is discreet and away from rest surfaces.
  • Service, controls and menus do not interrupt the shared period.

CFF application

Two-person stoneware, fire court, shared outward deck orientation and the aperture minute create attention without romantic styling. Acoustic privacy comes from mass, landscape and route separation.

ETHICAL LIMIT: design supports togetherness; it never assumes relationship, romance or consent

Validation

Speech privacy and interruption audit; seating/body-dimension mock-up; varied occupant-pair simulation; verify equivalent comfort for one occupant.

Failure / HOLD

Romantic cues are imposed; privacy requires closing off nature; one occupant controls the other’s comfort; seating excludes solo or non-couple configurations.

ES-007

Awe

Scale can exceed the self

Withholding

  • Do not reveal the dominant far prospect on arrival or everyday circulation.
  • Build visual appetite through near-field enclosure and controlled fragments.
  • Use one primary reveal; additional lookouts dilute the event.

Reveal geometry

  • Approach conceals the frame until the final turn.
  • Standing datum aligns with the far horizon and removes foreground clutter.
  • Frame proportion is established through full-scale optical study, not facade composition alone.

Safety & silence

  • Refuge and edge protection are resolved without visually domesticating the encounter.
  • No interpretive panel, soundtrack, brand mark or host narration.
  • Weather veto preserves physical safety and perceptual integrity.

CFF application

Harvest Ridge carries the estate’s only framed Western Ghats horizon. The guest reaches it on the final morning after days of ten-metre sightlines, luggage already gone, standing rather than seated.

AWE MECHANISM: contrast and timing, not the objective quality of a view alone

Validation

Seasonal view-cone study; full-scale aperture mock-up; approach-film analysis; safety review in fog, rain and wind; check that no earlier route leaks the horizon.

Failure / HOLD

Horizon visible prematurely; view becomes a room-category premium; sign or narration tells the occupant what to feel; frame competes with architecture branding.

ES-010

Restoration

Physiological recovery can occur

Sleep envelope

  • NC-25 at sleeping datum with all normal plant at 100% duty.
  • Field ASTC ≥65 between twins; drainage and hatch included in the path budget.
  • Blackout ≤1 lux; no visible diodes from pillow.

Thermal / air

  • Pillow velocity ≤0.1 m/s; RH 50–55% year-round.
  • CO₂ <800 ppm under design occupancy.
  • Thermal mass and radiant trim avoid perceptible cycling.

Circadian / duration

  • 4000 K dawn to 2200 K dusk; evening 50–80 lux; flicker <5%.
  • Protect at least three-night recovery logic in product and operations.
  • Pre-dawn ramp follows hamlet microclimate and sleep evidence.

CFF application

Cast mass, rain roof, acoustic spine, cluster DOAS, fog-specific desiccant capacity, predictive tub and thermal court operate as one recovery system. Architecture provides the conditions; research governs any physiological claim.

COMMISSIONING: G1/G2/G3/G4/G8/G9 must pass per hamlet; no averaging and no partial opening

Validation

24-hour monsoon soak, blower door, octave traces, field isolation, spectroradiometry, pillow air grid and consented research protocol. Never infer health benefit from operational telemetry alone.

Failure / HOLD

Any hamlet misses an environmental threshold; controls create sleep disturbance; physiological sensing lacks consent; recovery claim exceeds evidence grade.

ES-011

Gratitude

Value can be felt without assessment

Departure architecture

  • Route flows from sanctuary to horizon to gate without desk, queue or payment surface.
  • Luggage moves ahead through the operational network.
  • The final threshold closes quietly and does not demand acknowledgement.

Transactional absence

  • No price card, tip prompt, review request or sales display enters the spatial field.
  • Folio and legal obligations are resolved discreetly through the approved channel.
  • Staff do not stage a farewell to solicit emotion.

Material completion

  • The last touched object is place-specific and non-promotional.
  • No branded photo wall, retail threshold or exit merchandise.
  • Carryover object is given without a commercial explanation.

CFF application

The aperture is the last architectural sentence. The guest turns from the horizon and continues to the gate while luggage and settlement are already complete. The estate withholds its own name.

OBSERVATION: thanks may occur or not; absence of thanks is never treated as failure or scored

Validation

End-to-end departure rehearsal; count all asks, pauses and transaction surfaces; verify accessibility, legal settlement and recovery when digital settlement fails.

Failure / HOLD

Checkout desk, waiting vehicle confusion, visible bill dispute, staff farewell line, review solicitation or a retail display becomes the final memory.

ES-012

Anticipation

Return can remain imaginable

Deliberate incompleteness

  • Do not expose every hamlet, ritual, season or folly in one stay.
  • Protect seasonal differences as real conditions, not programmed themes.
  • Leave one meaningful route, clearing or state unconsumed.

Retrieval architecture

  • Object originates in an experienced material or ritual loop.
  • Home ritual is short, self-sufficient and free of engagement pressure.
  • Digital continuity is optional, consented and quiet.

Return pathway

  • One-tap return may preserve known tempo without forcing the same itinerary.
  • Physical system supports recognition and seasonal difference simultaneously.
  • No urgency, scarcity message, streak or reminder.

CFF application

The estate roast, river stone, two-minute ritual and home nocturne recall a real room condition. Blossom, fruiting and harvest give the next visit a materially different world. The Phantom remains undisclosed and unbookable.

SUCCESS MODEL: a return is invited by memory and incompleteness—not pursued by the estate

Validation

Provenance audit for every retrieval object; privacy and retention review; content audit against reminders and gamification; verify seasonal claims with estate operations.

Failure / HOLD

Every experience is exposed in marketing; carryover becomes merchandise; app uses behavioural pressure; the next season is claimed without ecological or operational truth.

Design authority conclusion: these twelve contracts must be resolved together. Optimising one state can damage another—privacy can become isolation, automation can erase agency, silence can remove sensory life, and awe can compromise safety. PVE-03 must therefore demonstrate system-level balance, not twelve independent checklists.
